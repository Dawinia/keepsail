## Findings

The plan provides a technically coherent bounded route for H1–H8 and does not reduce the architecture to a memory store, CI gate, or execution runtime. It keeps semantic execution with the harness and records/control with Keepsail. The selected Claude integration has explicit session and entry identity rather than adopting the most recently active historical Change. Its host-runtime behavior remains an acceptance dependency, not an assertion that hooks are already installed or active.

The authority chain is preserved. Effective commitments and restrictions come from the frozen approved subject, including complete artifact text where restrictions have no structured representation. Pending candidates remain separate. Ratified decisions retain their own identity, body hash and conformance process; the projection does not manufacture knowledge recognition for them. The plan leaves active policy replacement, new knowledge version recognition, dispositions, and commitment changes at their existing owner-controlled boundaries. It explicitly avoids extending the archived pilot selector or treating the owner's implementation confirmation as v1/v2 knowledge recognition.

The observable evidence design is adequate at plan level. The SessionStart hint is not acquisition. The observer requires a real CLI-return callback, a matching invocation, the correct root/Change/session/entry, and exact manifest/page/body correspondence. Partial pages cannot become whole-version acquisition: aggregation must reconstruct the complete returned version before invoking native `knowledge record`. Approval/convention/empty-set receipts remain separately identified outer context observations; they are not fake native knowledge versions. Agent statements and result checks remain distinct facts, and the disclosed boundary does not claim private understanding or semantic compliance.

The recovery/refresh design keeps historical supply and approval references while checking current policy, approval, knowledge and unresolved matters. It correctly excludes ordinary observation append operations from the authority invalidation key, avoiding an acquire-then-immediately-stale loop. Replayed delivery must reuse the retained original request rather than reuse an ID with a new CAS revision. Newly stale or incomplete pages remain evidence of past output without becoming current readiness.

The plan explicitly recognizes the existing `knowledge.check(paths=...)` limitation: local impact checking skips full-delivery enforcement. It therefore requires separate current supply and entry acquisition checks before applying action-local impact control and the original lifecycle authorization. Global/unknown dependence cannot be dismissed by the agent's independence claim; genuinely independent work and lawful authoring/recovery routes remain available. The shared pure gate and `gate check` use the same declarations in `gates.decisions`, preserving the ratified single-policy boundary and core layer direction.

The plan leaves Codex/default callers on their existing behavior and discloses shell/MCP/external-write coverage limits. The file list, lifecycle steps, exact-subject review requirements, and A1–A12 acceptance matrix are concrete enough to implement and review without reopening unrelated historical lifecycle work.
